Questions.

What kinds of books do you produce?

Any book with text: business, self-help, memoir, how-to, history, children's and every kind of fiction. Nonfiction usually gets one narrator, or your own voice. Novels can have a full cast.

Is it AI narration?

Yes. AI voices, directed line by line, with music and sound where it fits. Judge it on your own book: the sample is free.

Will it be on Audible?

Audible's rules on AI narration are stricter than most stores', so we don't promise it. Spotify, Apple Books and 20+ others accept it. If a store declines your title, we refund the $100 distribution part.

What do you take from royalties?

Nothing. You pay once per book. Everything the stores pay for your book goes to you.

Who owns it?

You do. It's non-exclusive, so you can sell it anywhere, and your book is never used to train AI.

How long does it take?

The sample usually arrives within a day. The full audiobook is usually ready within 24 hours of approving the voices.

Send us your Amazon link. We'll produce the opening and email it to you. Free, no card.
